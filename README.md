## What it is

Cross-lingual voice cloning for travelers and language learners. The user records 30 seconds of their voice on their own microphone, biometric-locked, no file uploads ever. Then they hear themselves speak 12 target languages with native pronunciation. Same voice, different tongue.

The wedge is the dated trip. Not "someday I'll learn Spanish." 30 days out, two weeks out, five days out. Help me not look helpless ordering coffee, asking for the bathroom, getting a cab in a city whose language I don't speak.

This is not ElevenLabs. ElevenLabs sells the technology to creators at $22/month. We sell the OUTCOME to travelers at $19 one-time, a curated trip pack, and the relief of sounding like yourself in a foreign city.

---

## How it works

Three taps. One read. One breath.

1. Pick where you are going. Tokyo, Rome, Madrid, Paris, Berlin, Lisbon, Seoul, Amsterdam, Beijing, Bangkok, Istanbul, Bali. More languages monthly, 19 supported by the model today and growing.
2. Read a 30-second paragraph into your microphone. The recording stays yours.
3. Hear yourself speak the language. Three seconds of silence. Then your voice. Native pronunciation. Your timbre.

## Stack

Built with TypeScript, Vite, React, Tailwind v4, Motion. Local MLX inference for voice synthesis (Qwen3-TTS, mlx-audio, MIT + Apache 2.0 licensed). Email magic-link auth, no password, no Google OAuth. Neon Postgres for account state. Cloudflare Tunnel for the API edge. No VC, no cloud lock-in, no surveillance-class telemetry.

## What we stand on

**Your voice. Never theirs.** Other apps clone celebrity voices, AI personas, generic actors. We clone you. The voice you trust most is the one that already lives in your head.

**Microphone only. Forever.** We never accept file uploads. The whole malicious-cloning attack surface is closed at the input gate. Biometric voiceprint locks each account to one voice.

**Built to delete itself.** 30-day trial purge by daily cron at 03:00 UTC. No grace period after cancellation. No re-engagement campaigns. Your voice exists on our servers only while you are actively using Idiolect.

**No guarantees we cannot keep.** AI is improving every day, we improve with it. We do not promise perfection, we promise effort. The only guarantee is the one we can keep: we delete your voice on the schedule above.
